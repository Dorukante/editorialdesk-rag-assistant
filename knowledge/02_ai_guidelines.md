# AI use guidelines (fictional demo)

## AI-assisted drafting
AI can assist with summaries and first drafts, but a human editor must verify all facts, references, and quotes before publication. AI-generated text must not be published without editorial review.

## Data privacy
Do not paste confidential interview notes, unpublished financial data, personal information, or subscriber records into unapproved external AI tools.

## AI transparency
Editors should disclose significant AI assistance to the responsible editor during the review process. The final editorial decision remains with a human.
