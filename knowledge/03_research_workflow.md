# Research workflow (fictional demo)

## Preparing research briefs
Analysts prepare short research briefs with a clear question, important figures, source links, and publication date. Each chart should identify its data source.

## Data quality checks
Before releasing a chart or analysis, analysts check missing values, duplicate records, and inconsistent date formats. Discrepancies must be resolved or clearly annotated.

## Updating recurring reports
Weekly reports are refreshed every Monday. The researcher checks whether input data was updated, reruns validation checks, and documents major methodological changes.
